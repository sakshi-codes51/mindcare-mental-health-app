Why one integrated platform?

Fragmented tools

Most apps only screen or only chat. MindCare brings screening, support, tracking and guidance together.

Recognition gap

Early warning signs are easy to miss. A short validated questionnaire helps you see where you stand.

Access gap

Cost, stigma and distance keep people from help. MindCare is free, private and available any time, with routes to free helplines.

No continuous view

Daily mood check-ins and charts show patterns over weeks, not just one bad day.

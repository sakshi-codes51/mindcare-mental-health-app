How MindCare works

The screening

The core questionnaire is the PHQ-4 (Kroenke, Spitzer, Williams & Löwe, 2009), a validated four-question screener for anxiety and depression. You rate how often each problem has bothered you over the last two weeks: 0 (not at all) to 3 (nearly every day).

  • Anxiety score = questions 1 + 2. Low-mood score = questions 3 + 4. A score of 3 or more on either is the standard cut-off for a positive screen.
  • Total (0–12) maps to: 0–2 Low Concern, 3–5 Moderate Concern, 6–12 High Concern. These follow the published PHQ-4 bands (normal, mild, moderate, severe), with moderate and severe grouped as High.
  • One extra safety question asks about thoughts of self-harm. Any answer above "not at all" always produces High Concern and shows crisis resources.
  • Six context questions (sleep, concentration, energy, overwhelm, loneliness, irritability) are not part of the validated score. They only personalise your suggestions.

The AI parts

  • Emotional-state analysis combines a transparent word-and-phrase engine (it understands things like "not happy" and "very anxious") with a small machine-learning model. It shows the words that influenced the result.
  • The chatbot checks every message for crisis language first, then responds with reflective listening and simple coping techniques. It runs on rules, not a large language model, and it is not a therapist.
  • Wellbeing and music suggestions are matched using only what you have told the app, such as your screening answers and journal entries.

Limits you should know about

  • Screening results are not diagnoses. Only a qualified professional can assess you.
  • Text analysis can be wrong, especially with sarcasm, slang or mixed languages.
  • MindCare is for adults (18+) and is not an emergency service.

Your privacy

  • Passwords are stored only as salted hashes. Sessions use signed, HTTP-only cookies.
  • Your data is visible only to your account. You can delete individual entries, or delete your whole account and everything in it from your profile at any time.
